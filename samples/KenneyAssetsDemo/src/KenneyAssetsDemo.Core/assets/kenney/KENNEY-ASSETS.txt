KENNEY ASSET BUNDLES - WHAT THEY ARE AND WHERE THEY CAME FROM
=============================================================

The two .zip files beside this note were created and distributed by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) and are
released under the Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal) public domain dedication:

    http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 content may be used in personal, educational and commercial projects and needs no attribution.
Kenney is credited here anyway, because crediting the source of an asset is the house style of this
repository. Each bundle also carries Kenney's own License.txt inside it, and the demo reads that
file: the licence title is what names the pack and what the pack slug in every asset key is made
from.

The bundles are shipped UNOPENED, exactly as they are downloaded, because reading a downloaded
bundle as it stands is the point of CodeBrix.Platform.GameEngine.KenneyAssets.

simulated_bundle.zip
    A small mixed bundle assembled from Kenney content: a Tiled map with four layers and two tile
    sets of different tile sizes, three animated glTF characters with external textures, three
    fonts, two SVG documents, loose tile images and a handful of sounds. Pack slug:
    simulated-bundle.

kenney_puzzle-pack-1.zip
    A 2D art pack: two sprite sheets with their TextureAtlas documents, the same art as loose
    images in two sizes, and a vector document. Pack slug: puzzle-pack.
